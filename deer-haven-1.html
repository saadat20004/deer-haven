Welcome to Deer Haven

Farm views, mountain air and a whole hillside of quiet.

Set up camp, then bike, hike or visit several lakes and waterfalls, all within about 6 miles. You are tucked into the back yard of the Catoctins, with Cunningham Falls, Greenbrier State Park and Catoctin Mountain Park close by and endless mountain back roads to explore.

Guests talk about the view over the fields, the sound of a little stream, and how calm it feels at night. It is a simple, peaceful alternative to crowded campgrounds.

Good to know

Simple camping, clearly explained.

This is rustic camping on private land. Here is exactly what is here and what to bring, so there are no surprises when you arrive.

What you get

  • Potable waterFresh drinking water on the property
  • Pets allowedDogs are welcome at every site
  • Private feeling sitesOpen hillside with room to spread out
  • A helpful hostShari shares local tips for hikes, climbing and swimming holes

Not available

  • No campfiresBring a camp stove for cooking
  • No toiletPlan a portable option
  • No electric hookupBring battery or solar power
  • No water hookupFill your tanks before arriving

Pack these

  • Leveling blocksThe ground has a gentle slope
